# Souffleur brand

**Name**: Souffleur. In the theatre, the prompter who hides in a small box at the edge of the stage and whispers the
next line. On a Mac, that box is the notch.
**Signature**: Your lines, right under the camera.

## Icon

A slab of black glass with the notch at its top edge, ringed with stage light, violet running into fuchsia, and the
script beneath it: the line being read lit by the same light, the lines to come fading away. Drawn by
`scripts/icon.swift` at every size of the asset catalog (`scripts/make-iconset.sh`), and at 1024 px in
`icon-1024.png`.

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Stage | `#050407` | The prompter, dark surfaces |
| Violet | `#8F6BFF` | The accent: buttons, selection, the level meter |
| Lavender | `#CDB9FF` | The next word and figures on black |
| Fuchsia | `#FF5AC8` | The other end of the stage light, gradients |
| Rose | `#FF8FB0` | The warmest note of the glow |

The glow around the notch is a conic gradient of violet, purple, fuchsia and rose that turns once every seven seconds
and brightens with the voice.

## Type

- Inside the app: the system font (SF Pro), and the prompter's font of choice (SF Pro, SF Pro Rounded, New York, SF
  Mono).
- Website: the system font, Inter where SF Pro is missing.

## Voice

Short, concrete, calm. Say what happens. No exclamation marks, no em dashes.
